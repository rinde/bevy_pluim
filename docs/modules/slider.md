# `slider.rs`

`Slider`: a themed horizontal range control over Bevy's headless slider.

Read this before changing `src/slider.rs`. When the module's behaviour changes, update this file in the same PR.

Cross-cutting design: [keyboard focus and navigation](../design/keyboard-focus.md).

## Details

`Slider::new(value, min, max)` and `with_step`, both returning `Result<_, SliderError>` (`SliderError`: `NonFiniteValue`, `NonFiniteBound`, `ReversedBounds`, `RangeTooWide`, `InvalidStep`; bounds must be finite with `min <= max` and a finite f32 `max - min`, a finite out-of-range value is clamped into the range, a step must be positive and finite and may exceed the range; `min == max` is valid and the value then never changes), with `value()`, `min()`, `max()` and `step()` as read-only getters (the fields are private), `with_format` (`SliderFormat`, `fn(f32) -> String`), `with_name` and `with_label_min_width`: a horizontal range control over Bevy's headless `bevy::ui_widgets::Slider` (`SliderValue`, `SliderRange`, `SliderStep`, `TrackClick::Snap`), 200 px wide unless the app gives its `Node` a width, 20 px high: a 4 px track in `slider_track`, a fill up to the value in `slider_fill` and a 16 px thumb of control radius (`card_surface` with a 2 px `slider_fill` border and the small shadow; `accent_subtle` hovered, `accent` pressed or dragged) that stays inside the slider at both ends (it moves in a `SliderTravel` node one thumb narrower, as the headless pointer maths assume). Its colours come from one function of disabled, pressed or dragged, and hovered (`look`); `InteractionDisabled` draws it in `text_disabled`, and the headless slider then ignores the pointer and keys. With a `format` its value is shown in a `SliderLabel` of at least `label_min_width` (54 px) in a right margin the slider reserves (a slider with a `format` owns its `margin.right`, set when the `Slider` is inserted; one without leaves it to the app), absolutely placed so that the track does not move as the text changes, secondary text in rem. A press on the track jumps the thumb there and a drag moves it, both through the headless slider's `ValueChange<f32>`, which a global observer snaps to the `step` (from `min`, clamped; `Slider::snap` computes in f64 so a tiny step cannot overflow, and gives `min` for a non-finite input) before the app's observers on the slider see it, then inserts as the `SliderValue`. One tab stop; while focused left / right move it by `step` (a hundredth of the range without one), Home / End to its ends (Bevy's), and `keep_navigation_keys` stops those keys from bubbling on (pressed, repeated or released); up and down go through. The focus ring is the `TabStop`'s. To a screen reader it is a `Role::Slider` (Bevy's numbers: value, bounds, step; `a_slider_is_a_slider_node_with_its_value_bounds_and_step`) whose `value` is the text `format` shows (none without one) and whose label is `name` (none by default). Fields are read when the component is inserted; an app sets the value by inserting a `SliderValue`. `SliderRefs` names the parts (a reflected component, like `ValueRef` and `UIntValue<T>`, which `tests/reflection.rs` checks). `restyle_sliders` visits only the sliders whose `Hovered`, `SliderValue` or `SliderDragState` changed or that gained `Pressed` or `InteractionDisabled`, plus those that lost either (`RemovedComponents` into a `Local` buffer, sorted and deduplicated, skipping those already styled), so an idle frame walks no slider and a slider that changes several ways at once is styled once (`a_slider_released_away_from_the_control_goes_back_to_rest`, `a_slider_follows_being_disabled_and_enabled_again`, `a_slider_follows_a_drag_and_a_value_change`, `a_slider_pressed_and_enabled_in_the_same_frame_ends_up_resting_and_enabled`, `a_theme_change_restyles_a_pressed_slider_and_idle_frames_leave_it_alone`; `sliders-idle` and `sliders-pressing` bench workloads, #698)

**Touch (#799):** a tap on the track jumps the thumb, a drag moves it, both snapped to the step; a disabled slider ignores a finger. The pressed look ends on lift and, through `end_cancelled_presses` (run when `Touches` reports a cancelled finger, because Bevy's headless slider only clears `Pressed` and its drag on release), on cancel. Scenarios: `slider_touch_tap_and_drag.ron`, `slider_touch_looks.ron`.
