# `tooltip.rs`

`Tooltip`: a popup shown after the pointer rests on a node.

Read this before changing `src/tooltip.rs`. When the module's behavior changes, update this file in the same PR.

Cross-cutting design: [popups](../design/popups.md).

## Details

`Tooltip { text }`: after the pointer (`Hovered`) rests on the node for `TOOLTIP_DELAY_SECS` (0.5 s) a `TooltipPopup` `Float` (`card_surface`, `border`, small `text_primary`) is spawned as a child of the node, hidden until laid out, then centerd under the node, kept 6 px inside the primary window and put above the node when it would run off the bottom, its `top` never above the 6 px margin; a `Tooltip` with empty text shows no popup, and a showing one goes when the text becomes empty; it never takes the pointer. Leaving the node or a `Pressed` hides it, and a press keeps it hidden until the pointer has left and returned. Only active tooltips are visited: `track_active_tooltips` puts the private `TooltipActive` marker on a node when its `Hovered` or `Tooltip` changes and it is hovered, `update_tooltips` queries only marked nodes and takes the marker off once the pointer is gone and no popup shows, so a tooltip the pointer is not on is not visited (a hovered one with empty text still is, while the pointer stays; the marker arrives through `Commands`, so a hover is first counted in the frame after it starts), guarded by the `tooltip.rs` unit tests `only_a_hovered_or_showing_tooltip_is_visited` and `an_unmarked_tooltip_is_not_visited_in_a_quiet_frame`. While a `NumericStepper` scrub drag holds the crate-private `HoverLock`, no tooltip shows or counts its delay and a showing one goes (`a_tooltip_stays_hidden_while_a_numeric_stepper_is_scrubbed`). `IconButton` inserts one with its name; the buttons of an underlined `Toolbar` (labels hidden) get one with their tool's label.

**Touch (#802).** A finger is not `Hovered`, so a touch has its own path. The crate-private `track_touch` observes `MouseEvent`: a touch `Pressed` on a `Tooltip` node or a descendant puts `TooltipActive` on the node and a `TouchPress { pointer, target, elapsed }` in its `TooltipClock` (the first finger wins; another finger's events are ignored). `update_tooltips` counts the time (not while `HoverLock` is on, which also resets it) and shows the popup, marked `TouchPopup`, once the finger has been down `TOOLTIP_DELAY_SECS` and has not moved more than `DRAG_THRESHOLD` (3 px, the stepper's, now `crate::DRAG_THRESHOLD`) from where it went down: a `Drag` past it before the popup shows, a `Released`, a `Cancel` or an `Out` of the pressed entity ends the touch, and the popup goes with it. **Suppression:** the moment a touch popup shows, `end_press` removes `Pressed` from the pressed entity up to the tooltip node and triggers a `MouseKind::Cancel` `MouseEvent` on each (not marked as a finger's, so `track_touch` does not take it for the finger leaving), so the pressed look goes back to resting and Bevy's `Button`, which fires `Activate` on a click only while `Pressed`, fires none on the lift. `IconButton` (and its `ToggleState`), the toolbar's `toggle_selected` and the segmented control's `set_selection` all act on `Activate`, so they pick nothing; an app's `Tooltip` on a `Button` is the same. A short tap (lift before the delay) shows nothing and activates as a tap does. **Placement:** `place_popups` puts a touch popup above the node when it fits inside the margin (the finger covers what is below), else as before (below, or above when below runs off the bottom). The marker is removed once the touch is gone, the node is not hovered and no popup shows (`a_touch_is_visited_only_while_it_is_down_or_its_popup_shows`, `a_short_tap_or_a_moving_touch_shows_no_popup`, `only_the_first_finger_counts_and_a_cancel_ends_it`). Scenarios: `tooltip_touch_long_press.ron` (Tooltip screen: shows, hides on lift and cancel, no `Activate`, no toggle flip, short tap, moving finger), `tooltip_touch_long_press_toolbar.ron` (the underlined toolbar's label above the button, nothing picked), `tooltip_touch_long_press_segmented_control.ron` (icon-only segment).
