# `panel.rs`

`Panel`: a card with a header, optional close button, strip, scrolling body and footer.

Read this before changing `src/panel.rs`. When the module's behavior changes, update this file in the same PR.

## Details

`Panel`: a `Card` 380 px wide with a header (title `Label`, optional subtitle beside it, optional close `IconButton` that triggers the `PanelClosed` entity event on the panel), a strip, a `Divider`, a body in a `ScrollArea` that grows with its content up to `max_body_height` (620) and then scrolls, and a footer above a divider. The app spawns contents as children of the entities in `PanelRefs` (`strip()`, `body()`, `footer()`); an empty strip or footer is `Display::None`. `Panel` is an immutable component (`&mut Panel` does not compile, guarded by `compile_fail` doctests): insert a new one to change title, subtitle, close icon, `close_name` or maximum height; the parts are updated in place (no `Changed<Panel>` sync), the app's children in strip, body and footer stay, and the close observer is registered once, so `PanelClosed` comes once per activation (`inserting_a_panel_again_keeps_its_parts_content_and_one_close_notification`). `close_name` (`with_close_name`, default "Close", `PanelTemplate::close_name`) is the accessible name of the close `IconButton` (and its tooltip); it does not decide whether the button shows, `close_icon` does (`a_panels_close_button_is_named_close_unless_the_app_names_it`). `PanelRefs` is public, queryable and immutable with private fields and getters `header()`, `title()`, `subtitle()`, `close()`, `strip()`, `scroll_area()`, `body()`, `footer()` (a `compile_fail` doctest guards the private fields); only the widget builds it. Migrating (nopjes): `Query<&mut Panel>` / `get_mut::<Panel>` become an insert of a prepared `Panel`, and `refs.body` and the like become `refs.body()`. `max_body_height` is private (read it with `max_body_height()`) and must be finite and above zero: `with_max_body_height(h)` returns `Result<Panel, PanelHeightError>` and rejects NaN, infinities, zero and negatives without clamping, and `PanelTemplate`'s public `f32` field is checked by the same helper in `build_template`, so a `bsn!` `Panel { max_body_height: .. }` with a bad value is a template error (`spawn_scene` returns `Err`) and builds no panel (`a_panel_scene_with_an_invalid_maximum_body_height_fails_to_build_and_spawns_no_panel`). For `max_body_height`, replace field reads with `max_body_height()` and add `?` / `.unwrap()` handling to `with_max_body_height`. A scroll area clips to a rectangle, which cannot follow the card's rounded corners, so with no footer the body's scroll area has a bottom margin of `panel_radius` and ends above the corners (its `max_height` is `max_body_height` plus that margin; `sync_optional_sections` keeps both, `panel_body_stays_clear_of_rounded_corners.ron`). The body is `should_block_lower: false`, or it would cover the scroll contents and the wheel would never reach them. Grow-then-scroll needs only `max_height` on the `ScrollArea` node (`a_panel_body_grows_with_its_content_up_to_its_maximum_and_then_scrolls`). It is a `Role::Group` (from `Card`) with no label of its own that is `labeled_by` its title `Label` (so a new `Panel` needs no relabeling); the close button keeps its own name (`panel_accessible_group.ron`). `sync_optional_sections` looks only at panels whose `Panel` or `PanelRefs` changed or whose strip or footer `Children` changed (the private `PanelSection(panel)` marker on both names the panel), all of them on a `Theme` change (`a_panel_keeps_its_sections_and_corner_margin_across_theme_changes_and_quiet_frames`; `panels-idle` and `panels-changing` bench workloads, #685)
