# `tab_bar.rs`

`TabBar`: Bevy's tab list over a selection of `Item<String>`.

Read this before changing `src/tab_bar.rs`. When the module's behaviour changes, update this file in the same PR.

Cross-cutting design: [keyboard focus and navigation](../design/keyboard-focus.md), [lists and selection](../design/lists-and-selection.md).

## Details

`TabBar`: Bevy's `bevy_ui_widgets` `TabList` of text `TabBarTab`s (Bevy `Tab`s) over a `Selection` of `Item<String>`, own or `Selection::Ref`, normally a `One`, styled from the `segmented_*` slots like a `SegmentedControl`. The selected look is a `TabIndicator` (a crate-private child of the bar from `indicator.rs`, as for a `SegmentedControl`) that slides to the selected tab when the selection changes; the tabs' own backgrounds are transparent, and `order_tabs` keeps the indicator after the tabs. `SelectedTab` follows the `Selection`, only of the bars whose own or referenced selection changed (`selection_change` reads `Changed<Selection>` and `SelectionParam::get_ref`, so an unrelated bar is not restyled; `a_tab_bar_follows_only_the_selection_it_draws_from`), which keeps the last word: Bevy's `tablist_self_update` is not registered, and the `ValueChange<Option<Entity>>` the tab list sends for a click, Enter or Space goes through `SelectionParam::update_selection` (`set_selection`). The tabs are not `Button`s (a `Button` stops the click before the tab list sees it) nor `TabStop`s: Bevy's `TabPlugin` (added by `WidgetsPlugin` unless the app has it) gives the selected or focused tab `TabIndex` 0 and the others -1, so the bar is one tab stop and the arrows, Home and End move the focus between its tabs. A hidden bar (itself or an ancestor `Display::None`, as in a hidden `Stack` panel, or hidden visibility) gives all its tabs -1, so Tab skips it, and the roving 0 comes back when it is shown: `clear_dangling_tab_bars` clears the tabs of a bar whose source was despawned, as for a `SegmentedControl`, and the bar recovers when re-pointed (`a_tab_bar_is_cleared_when_the_source_behind_its_selection_is_despawned`). `sync_tab_index` in `tab_bar.rs` runs after Bevy's and after visibility propagation and keeps both, looking only at bars that changed (new, `SelectedTab`, `Children`, a tab's `InteractionDisabled` or `TabIndex`, a focus move to or from one of its tabs, or the shared `HiddenDirty` of visibility, `Display::None` and parent; `tab-bars-idle` bench workload, #670), with `focus.rs`'s `is_hidden` (`a_hidden_tab_bar_is_not_reached_by_tab_and_is_again_once_shown`, `tab_skips_a_tab_bar_in_a_hidden_stack_panel`). Changing the text of an `Item<String>` updates its tab's text in place (`sync_label_text`), and inserting or reordering items puts the tabs in the items' order without respawning the others (`order_tabs`, linear through `select.rs`'s `items_in_order`; `on_items_changed` reconciles with a set of wanted and a set of shown items, a second tab of one item is despawned, #679) (`changing_an_items_text_updates_its_tab_in_place`, `an_item_inserted_in_the_middle_puts_its_tab_there`, `reordering_items_reorders_their_tabs`). A tab's `TextFont` is written only when it differs, so a hover or selection restyle relays nothing out (#710). To a screen reader the bar is a `Role::TabList` and each tab a `Role::Tab` labelled with its item's text, `selected` while it is the `SelectedTab` (`tab_bar_accessible_tab_list.ron`)

Touch: a tap picks as a click does, a finger that slides off before lifting picks nothing, and no hover look stays after the lift (a finger never hovers); the focus, and so the roving stop, follows the tapped item (`tab_bar_touch_tap.ron`).
