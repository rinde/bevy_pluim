# `scroll_area.rs`

`ScrollArea`: scrolling contents with themed scroll bars, the wheel handler and scroll-into-view.

Read this before changing `src/scroll_area.rs`. When the module's behaviour changes, update this file in the same PR.

Cross-cutting design: [scrolling](../design/scrolling.md).

## Details

`ScrollArea::bundle` (for code that spawns through `Commands`) and `ScrollArea::scene(contents, vertical, horizontal)`, the same area as a scene whose `contents` is a factory, `impl Fn() -> impl SceneList + Send + Sync + 'static` (`@ScrollArea::scene(move || bsn_list! { .. }, true, false)` in a `bsn!`; it inserts the bundle after the scene is applied). The factory runs for every build of the template, so each area, also from one resolved scene spawned again, gets fresh contents and its own scroll bar targets, with nothing consumed on the first build; a captured handle or data is cloned inside the closure (`a_scroll_area_scene_gives_every_build_its_own_fresh_contents`, `a_cached_scroll_area_scene_builds_fresh_contents_each_time_it_is_spawned`, `a_scroll_area_factory_with_a_captured_handle_builds_asset_scenes_again_and_again`), `ScrollContents`, the `ScrollBar` marker (a zero-sized immutable component, reflected; the axis is not stored on it but is the `orientation` of the `bevy_ui_widgets` `Scrollbar` on the same entity, which grid cell, thickness, visibility and theming all read; the crate has no `Orientation` of its own; its `on_insert` hook is private and queues one idempotent command that reuses the bar's `ScrollThumb` or spawns exactly one, so inserting it again, also twice before the commands are applied, never duplicates it; `refresh_changed_scroll_bars` re-places and re-sizes a live bar when its `Scrollbar` changes; theming touches only the marked thumb, other children of the bar are the app's; the thumb's background is one function of its state, `thumb_background(colors, hovered, pressed)` (`scroll_thumb_background` at rest, `hovered_item` hovered, `selected_hovered_item` pressed or dragged), called by the pointer handler, by the restyle on a `ScrollbarDragState` change (a drag that ends away from the thumb) and by `theme_scroll_area`, so a theme change under the pointer keeps the colour (`scroll_area_thumb_colours.ron`); the thumb requires picking's `Hovered` and gets Bevy's `Pressed` while held; a bar without a `Scrollbar` does nothing) and the thumb, themed here; each bar is also a `bevy_ui_widgets` `Scrollbar` aimed at the contents and its thumb a `ScrollbarThumb` (no `Node`), so the widget sizes and places the thumb, scrolls the contents when it is dragged and pages them on a click on the track, while `show_scroll_bars` hides a bar whose axis fits, looking again when the contents are laid out or the bar's `Scrollbar` changes, so a live orientation change shows or hides the bar by its new axis (`changing_the_headless_orientation_shows_or_hides_the_bar_by_its_new_axis`; a child of a bar that is not the `ScrollThumb` is the app's and is left alone by theming and by inserting `ScrollBar` again, `an_app_owned_child_of_a_scroll_bar_survives_theming_and_reinserting_the_marker`); and the crate-private `update_scroll_position`, which scrolls any `ScrollPosition` under the pointer with the wheel (the targets are found once per frame, not per message, in buffers kept in a `Local`, and each message is then applied and clamped in turn, never summed; a `ScrollPosition` is written only when the clamped result differs, so a wheel at a limit or with no delta along an axis does not mark it changed or close a `Combo` in the area (`a_wheel_that_cannot_move_a_scroll_area_does_not_mark_its_position_changed`, `a_wheel_at_the_limit_leaves_an_open_combo_in_the_scroll_area_open`); a frame with no message returns at once, #682) (an area that scrolls sideways only takes a vertical turn, which is all a mouse wheel gives, for a horizontal one: `scroll_area_wheel_vertical_turn_scrolls_horizontal_area.ron`, the `image_gallery` example) Wheel events reach the nearest scrollable ancestor of a hovered control, stopping at a `FixedNode`; over a nested area only the innermost one scrolls and nothing is passed on at a limit: `scroll_area_wheel_nested_horizontal_in_vertical.ron`. An area clips to a rectangle (Bevy 0.20 has no rounded clip), so one at the bottom of a rounded `Card` shows contents sticking out of the card's corners: insert the opt-in `ClearOfCorners` with the area and it gets a bottom margin of `panel_radius`, kept in step with the `Theme`, and removing it sets the bottom margin back to `Val::ZERO` (an area cannot tell whether it touches the corners, so it is not automatic; `Panel` does the same for its own body, only without a footer; `scroll_area_in_card_stays_clear_of_rounded_corners.ron` on the `ScrollAreas` screen). `PopupScrollBar { list }` (crate-private) is the bar of a list that scrolls itself, a `Combo` or `FilterCombo` popup capped to the window: `popup_scroll_bar(list)` spawns a `ScrollBar` + `Scrollbar` (vertical, aimed at the list) as a child of the list, and `place_popup_scroll_bars` (`PostUpdate`, before layout) keeps it at the visible top and right edge of the list (`top` = the list's `ScrollPosition`, since an absolute child scrolls with its content), as high as the list, hidden while the rows fit; `theme_popup_scroll_bars` themes it like an area's (`theme_bar`, shared). A bar the pointer is on counts as inside the popup for the outside-click rule (`popup_scroll_bar_hovered`; `combo_popup_scroll_bar.ron`). Put a `List` or palette in one to scroll it. `ScrollContents` has 3 px of padding on every side (`FOCUS_RING_REACH`) so a focus ring at the edge is not clipped, and `scroll_into_view` counts it as part of the entity. The area is a `Role::ScrollView`; its bars and thumb have no accessibility node (`scroll_area_accessible_scroll_view.ron`)

**Touch (#799):** a finger drag on a scroll bar thumb scrolls, a tap on the track pages, and the thumb returns to its rest colour after the lift. Scenarios: `scroll_area_touch_thumb_drag.ron`, `_tap_pages.ron`, `_looks.ron`.
